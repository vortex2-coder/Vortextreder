LEARN BEFORE YOU TRADE

Guides

Short notes for using the manual terminal safely.

Connect an account

Use the Deriv authorization button, review the requested permissions, and return to Vortextreder. Verify the displayed account ID and type before requesting quotes.

Read digit statistics

Digit frequencies describe only the sample observed. They are historical summaries, not forecasts, and do not change the probability of the next independent tick.

Review a manual quote

Choose a market and contract, request a quote, check its expiry and maximum price, and submit only if you intend to. Real-account orders use real money.

Use a session loss limit

Set limits before trading and stop when a limit is reached. Do not raise stakes to recover losses; no strategy removes the risk of losing your stake.
